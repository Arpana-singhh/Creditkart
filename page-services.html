A home loan is a financial arrangement by bank or a lender to an individual to purchase a residential property.

Home Loan - Low interest rate home loans from a wide range of lenders

A home loan is an amount an individual borrows from bank or financial institution to buy a new/resale home, construct a home or renovate the home.

Benefits and Features of Home Loan

Tax Benefits: On Home Loan you can claim tax benefits under the Income Tax Act, 1961. As per Section 80C, you can claim up to INR 1.5 lakh on principal repayments, and up to INR 2 lakh on interest repayments under Section 24B.

Interest Rates: As compared to the other types of loans, the interest rate on a Home Loan is relatively lower.

High Repayment Tenure: Among all types of loans, a Home Loan has the longest repayment tenure, which goes up to 30 years. This helps reduce the burden of Equated Monthly Installments (EMIs) by extending the tenure.

No Prepayment Penalty: In a Home Loan, prepayments are not charged when you take a floating-rate Home Loan. This helps you close the loan before the set tenure.

Home Loan Interest Rates

Home loan interest rates start from 8.15% p.a. The rate offered depends on the loan type, tenure, financial profile and credit history. A stronger credit profile can help you qualify for a lower rate.

How to apply for a Home Loan @Livanity:

1. Fill the Application form:

The customer needs to fill in the Loan Application form which includes details such as Full Name (As per PAN Card), Email Address, Current residential address, employment details, current company name, monthly in-hand salary, mobile Number, age, income, employment stability, and credit score ensure that you meet their requirements.

2. Documentation:

Once you fill the loan application form, you will have to attach the following documents for verification:

  • Identity proof - PAN card/ Aadhar Card / Voter ID/ Driving license
  • Address of proof - can be a copy of any utility bill
  • Salary slips of the last 3 months
  • Proof of employment
  • Bank statements of the last 6 months
  • Form 16

3. Processing and Verification:

After submission of the application form along with the required documents, the bank starts processing the Home Loan application. The bank will verify all the documents provided by you. A bank representative may even visit your workplace or home or both for the same.

The bank conducts an extensive enquiry into your credit score to check your creditworthiness as a borrower. If all your documents are in order and you have a satisfactory credit score and report, the bank will proceed with your loan application.

4. Sanction Letter:

On successful approval of your loan application, the bank will send a sanction letter to the borrower. This letter acts as proof that the bank has approved your loan. All essential details about the loan transaction are enclosed in this letter, such as:

  • The loan amount you are eligible for
  • The interest rate offered
  • Whether the interest rate is fixed or variable
  • Tenure of repayment
  • Terms and conditions of repayment

5. Secure Payment Fee:

On signing the sanction letter, the client will have to pay a one-time secure payment fee which may be payable upfront either before or after the loan sanction.

6. Legal and Technical Check:

Before the loan is disbursed, the bank will conduct legal and technical checks. The bank representatives will verify the property for which the loan is applied and check the ownership rights of the property.

During the technical check, the bank representatives will evaluate the actual value of the property. The status of the property - under construction or resale - will also be taken into consideration.

In case of an under construction property, they will check the construction stage and quality of the work. Whereas, if the property is a resale one, the bank will check the age and maintenance of the property.

In case of resale, the bank may also check if the property has already been mortgaged before or not.

7. Loan Disbursal:

Once the legal and technical checks are completed and the bank is satisfied with the checks conducted, the loan application will be approved and the client will receive a final agreement letter.

Prior to disbursal of loan, the borrower will receive a welcome kit with detailed housing loan EMI schedule.

If the borrower wants to switch the lender, there is an option for the Home Loan Transfer procedure.
