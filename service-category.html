Home Loan Services

Home Loan for Salaried

A home loan is an amount an individual borrows from bank or financial institution to buy a new/resale home, construct a home or renovate the home.

Get a Quote

You can get a home loan whether your need is big or small. We finance all of the following:

  • Under-construction, ready-for-possession or builder properties
  • New properties or resale properties
  • Properties from state housing boards, cooperative housing societies, apartment owners' associations, development authorities and private developers
  • Properties in cities, regularised colonies and gram panchayat areas
  • Multi-unit or self-constructed properties, construction on your own plot, or refinancing of a residential property
  • Construction on freehold or leasehold plots, including plots allotted by a development authority

Eligibility for Home Loan for Salaried

  • Salaried individuals

1. Nationality

Indian residents and non-resident Indians.

2. Age (Primary Applicant)

23 to 60 years for resident Indians, and 25 to 60 years for NRIs.

3. Eligible Job Profiles

People working in proprietorships, partnerships, LLPs, private or public-sector companies and MNCs.

4. Co-owned Property

A co-applicant may help secure a better interest rate. Adding a spouse or mother to the home loan can help you access a lower rate, even if they are not earning.

Adding a co-applicant can increase home-loan eligibility and may help you qualify for a larger amount. A co-applicant may be your spouse or an immediate family member.
