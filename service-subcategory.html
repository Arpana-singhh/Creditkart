Home Loan for Salaried

Indusind Bank offers various home loan products tailored to meet different needs, such as purchasing a new home, constructing a home, or transferring an existing home loan.

Following are the Indusind Bank Home Loan products:

  • Balance Transfer
  • Top-up Loan
  • Plot plus Construction Loan
  • Home Loans to NRI
  • Home Renovation/ Extension Loan
  • Approved Project Home Loan
  • Purchase of New Home

Here are some key aspects of Indusind Bank Home Loan:

1. Loan Amount: The bank offers home loans ranging from small amounts to substantial sums, depending on the borrower's eligibility and the value of the property.

2. Interest Rates: Competitive interest rates are available on fixed or floating terms. The specific rate may vary based on the loan amount, tenure and the applicant's credit profile.

3. Loan Tenure: Flexible tenure options are available, typically up to 30 years, allowing for manageable EMIs.

4. Processing Fee: A processing fee may apply, often as a percentage of the loan amount or as a fixed fee.

5. Prepayment and Foreclosure: Prepayment and foreclosure options are generally available, sometimes with minimal or no extra charges.

6. Top-Up Loan: Existing home loan customers may have the option to avail a top-up loan for additional financial needs.

Eligibility Criteria for Loan

1. Age: Usually, applicants should be between 21 and 65 years.

2. Income: A stable income source, either from employment or business, is required.

3. Credit Score: A good credit score, typically 750 and above, is preferred.

4. Employment: Salaried employees, self-employed professionals and business owners can apply.

Documentation Required for Loan

1. Identity Proof: Aadhaar card, PAN card, passport, voter ID, etc.

2. Address Proof: Utility bills, rent agreement, passport, etc.

3. Income Proof: For salaried applicants: salary slips, Form 16 and bank statements. For self-employed applicants: financial statements, ITRs and bank statements.

4. Property Documents: Agreement to sale, title deed, approved building plan, etc.

5. Other Documents: Passport-sized photographs, application form and any other documents requested by the bank.

Interest Rates

Interest rates vary based on the loan amount, tenure and the applicant's credit profile. Indusind Bank Home Loan provides competitive rates in line with market standards.
